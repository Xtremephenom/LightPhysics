# ADR-0011 — SQLite-backed in-process jobs

Status: accepted (pre-decided)
Date: 2026-10-18
Phase: 0

## Context

Long-running work needs queueing, progress, cancellation, crash recovery, and resource decisions without a broker service.

## Options considered

- SQLite-backed journal and in-process scheduler with a Resource Governor.
- External queue/broker.
- Unjournaled child processes.

## Evidence

Pre-decided in `docs/pack/03_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2 and §6, ADR 011. Crash-resume and process-tree tests belong to Phase 1.

## Decision

Use a SQLite-backed job journal and in-process scheduler. Supervise child processes and make jobs resumable or explicitly orphaned.

## Consequences

Deployment remains local and small. Process lifecycle, idempotency, and recovery are first-class responsibilities.

## How to revert

A measured throughput or recovery failure may motivate a replacement, but it must preserve the journal and typed job semantics.
